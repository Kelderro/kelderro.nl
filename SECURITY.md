# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you find a security issue in [kelderro.nl](https://kelderro.nl) or in this repository, please report it privately by email to [security@kelderro.nl](mailto:security@kelderro.nl). Do not open a public issue.

Please include:

- the affected URL or file,
- steps to reproduce, and
- the impact you expect.

You can write in English or Dutch. I aim to reply within five working days.

The same contact details are published in [`/.well-known/security.txt`](https://kelderro.nl/.well-known/security.txt) ([RFC 9116](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9116)).
